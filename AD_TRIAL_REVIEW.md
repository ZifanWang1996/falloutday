# FalloutDay — Advertising Trial Review Checklist

**Scope:** only the labelled EffectiveCPM placement after the FAQ on `/fo76-guides/first-50-levels/`.

## Stop immediately if any of these occur

- Popups, popunders, forced new tabs, automatic redirects, or download prompts.
- Sexual, gambling, deceptive, malware-like, illegal, or otherwise unsuitable creative.
- A sticky or oversized placement that blocks guide content, especially on mobile.
- Console errors that prevent reading or navigation.
- Layout shift that moves the article while a reader is trying to use it.

## Per-review checks

1. Open the article directly in a desktop browser and a narrow mobile viewport.
2. Confirm the **Sponsored placement** disclosure appears before the ad container.
3. Confirm the placement is after FAQ/content and before the footer.
4. Navigate to the homepage, Privacy, and Terms pages; confirm no EffectiveCPM script is present.
5. Check the console for errors and check the page for unexpected navigation.
6. In Plausible, compare page views/exit behavior for the trial article against other guides.
7. In the ad dashboard, record impressions, fill rate (if shown), earnings, and any policy notices.

## Expansion rule

Do not add a second placement until at least 7 days of clean behavior and measurable data. If expanded, use a second post-content slot on one different long-form guide only; never place ads in navigation, the homepage hero, Privacy, Terms, or inside instructional steps.
