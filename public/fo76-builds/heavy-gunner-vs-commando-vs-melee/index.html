Heavy Gunner, Commando, or Melee? A Time-and-Resource Comparison

The useful question is not which playstyle wins a universal tier list. It is which one fits your normal session length, preferred range, resource comfort, and willingness to manage mistakes.

Last reviewed: July 2026 · Independent fan commentary

Commando: flexible range, steady attention

Commando can suit players who enjoy ranged pacing and frequent small decisions. Test whether ammunition pressure, AP use, and aim demands remain comfortable across a full session—not only during a short easy encounter.

Heavy Gunner: direct output, bigger resource questions

Heavy Gunner may feel good for players who prefer a clear, committed combat rhythm. Before choosing it, check whether its resource and carry-weight demands fit how you actually play and resupply.

Melee: close-range commitment, simple resource loop

Melee can reduce some ammunition pressure, but it asks you to manage distance, positioning, and mistakes more directly. It is a good fit when close-range play feels enjoyable rather than stressful.

Choose by your limiting factor

  • Prefer distance and flexible pacing? Start by comparing Commando with your current resource comfort.
  • Prefer a heavier, committed combat rhythm? Test Heavy Gunner in a normal-length session.
  • Prefer low ammunition dependence and do not mind closing distance? Test Melee in a familiar activity.

Frequently asked questions

Which is best for a casual player?

The best starting point is the one whose maintenance and learning curve fit your available time. Test one approach in a repeatable activity before rebuilding around it.

Should I change playstyles for a small damage increase?

Only if the new style also fits your range preference, resource loop, and ability to recover from mistakes. A sustainable build is usually more useful than a short-lived peak.
