About FalloutDay

FalloutDay is a fan-run editorial project for players who want practical Fallout 76 guidance without pretending that one build is right for everyone.

Editorial promise

We distinguish official information, testing observations, and editorial recommendations. Version-sensitive guidance is reviewed and dated, and corrections are welcome.
