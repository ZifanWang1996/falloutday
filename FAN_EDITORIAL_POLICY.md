# FalloutDay — Fan Editorial & IP Policy

## Identity statement
Place this statement in the site footer and About page:

> falloutday.online is an unofficial fan commentary site and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Bethesda Softworks, ZeniMax Media, or Microsoft.

## Editorial rules
- Write original analysis, explanations, checklists, tables, and conclusions.
- Separate official facts, personal testing observations, and editorial recommendations.
- Time-sensitive claims must show a review date, context, and official source when available.
- Provide a correction and copyright-contact path before ads are enabled.

## Brand and materials restrictions
- Use brand names only as necessary to identify the game in commentary or compatibility descriptions.
- Never claim official status, approval, partnership, or sponsorship.
- Do not use official logos, cloned official visual identity, copied game text, copied Wiki/competitor text, data dumps, game files, audio, 3D models, or mass screenshot galleries.
- Prefer original text, original diagrams, licensed stock/illustration, and carefully reviewed minimal commentary imagery.

## Advertising gate
Advertising stays disabled until the site has:
1. Original useful content and clear editorial ownership.
2. Privacy, Terms, disclosures, and a visible public contact alias.
3. Ad placement rules that exclude build decisions, critical guide steps, navigation, and mobile overlays.
4. Production QA for layout shift, unexpected redirects, privacy disclosure accuracy, and mobile reading.
