## Owner action (Google Search Console)

1. Open <https://search.google.com/search-console/> while signed into the Google account you want to own the property.
2. Click **Add property** → choose **URL prefix** → enter exactly: `https://falloutday.online/`.
3. Complete Google’s offered verification method. Do not send any verification token, password, or code in chat.
4. Open **Sitemaps** in the left menu → paste only: `sitemap.xml` → click **Submit**.

After this, Google may take days or weeks to crawl and index a new site. Submission requests discovery; it does not guarantee immediate ranking or indexing.

## First review metrics

- Page views and entry pages.
- Whether the advertising trial page has unusual bounce/exit behavior.
- Any crawl or indexing warnings.
